# tl21 — the tiers (a verdict job, and prose runs nothing)

Wave 53, contract `sprints/tooling/tl21-the-compiler-tiers.md` (planning
repo `wolffe-lang/wolf`, read 2026-10-13), with the rules of
`sprints/wave-53.md` down to `wave-45.md`. Branch `tl21` from trunk
`d4a96e8`. The same lane lands the tiers in wolf-lang; pax has had them
since px37. §1 to §3 are committed before the first change.

One deliverable here: is75's event-level plan gains (a) a last job named
`tier full`, `tier pr` or `tier noop` that fails unless the plan succeeded,
every job the plan chose succeeded and every other job was skipped, and
(b) the prose-only rule. The pull-request leg is not changed.

## 1. Forbidden

- No change to what a job checks: no step dropped, no assertion moved, no
  job out of the full matrix. The pull-request leg (ubuntu's three shards
  and ladder, the two smokes, shard coverage, independence, dist floor)
  runs what it ran. `release.yml` is not touched.
- Prose is narrow: a path is prose only if no test, no gate and no archive
  step reads it. `README.md`, `docs/manual/` and `docs/repl.md` are read
  (`tests/doc_truth.rs`, `tests/repl_session.rs`, the bundle export) and
  are not prose. A path no rule names, and a diff that cannot be read, run
  the full matrix.
- The verdict job runs on `!cancelled()`, never `always()`; every `needs`
  output is read with a default; a run that is no pull request's has a
  concurrency group of its own run id.
- The planner's test is seen red in a CI run of its own, on a branch that
  is never merged, before it is trusted. "Seen red" cites the run.
- No build on nomad-1 (the planner and its test are scripts). No `rm`
  outside `~/lanes/tl21/`; no scratch in `/tmp`; no `git add -A`; nothing
  under `~/.claude`; no `2>/dev/null` on a checkout; no attribution
  trailer.
- No merge, tag or release; no issue filed or closed (listed instead). No
  run cancelled that is not this lane's own. The PR is opened without the
  `full-matrix` label, and the label goes on only at the final head.
