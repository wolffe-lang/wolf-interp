The std pin, a ruling (recommended, never decided here)
-------------------------------------------------------
(A) Re-pin to be1d10fa with wolf-std#147 named as a limit of the row on
macOS, not of the library: the release ships sc81's sessions and
pseudo-terminals and sc82's std.poll in its narrow form, and the row and
the std's doc are fixed in wolf-std (the row's child should be one that
takes no terminal, or the row should say a shell may take one).
(B) Keep 2411d868: neither sc81 nor sc82 ships, and burrow's terminal
request and the multiplexer wait another release for them.
(C) Hold the release for a runtime change to os_spawn_session on macOS.
Recommended: (A). The probe shows the spawn gives no controlling
terminal; nothing in the runtime is at fault, so (C) would change
nothing, and the std's only red is a row whose child is bash on macOS.
